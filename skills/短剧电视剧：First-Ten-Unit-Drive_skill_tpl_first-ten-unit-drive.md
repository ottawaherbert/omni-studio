## 简介
Serial script pattern for entry, expansion, and first-stage goal formation.
## 适用信息
- 默认范围：1-10
- 强度：medium
- 优先级：14
## 使用规则
Serial script pattern for entry, expansion, and first-stage goal formation.
## 结构节拍
### 1-3 entry
- 目标：Hook and first return.
- 必须包含：Core viewing promise.；First concrete payoff.
- 避免：Do not delay the central pleasure beyond unit three.
- 结尾钩子：Open a wider field or stronger opposition.
- 验收：The first three units cannot be pure explanation.

### 4-10 stage goal
- 目标：Expand pressure and form a stage target.
- 必须包含：Escalating opposition.；A target that can be won or lost.
- 避免：Do not let side material detach from the main pressure.
- 结尾钩子：Build toward a stage climax or strong question.
- 验收：The viewer should see a next-stage chase by unit ten.
## 全局必须
- The first ten units should prove the premise can sustain serial momentum.
## 全局避免
- Do not repeat the same conflict layer without changing stakes.
## 验收规则
- A measurable stage goal should exist by the tenth unit.
## 使用边界
- 这是创作方法参考，不覆盖项目事实、目标集数、目标时长和输出格式。
- 根据当前作品形态和具体任务灵活取用，不要生搬硬套。
