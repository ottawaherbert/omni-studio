## 简介
Script opening pattern for a strong hook, core play, and first payoff.
## 适用信息
- 默认范围：1-3
- 强度：medium
- 优先级：18
## 使用规则
Script opening pattern for a strong hook, core play, and first payoff.
## 结构节拍
### Unit 1 hook
- 目标：Establish protagonist pressure and a reason to continue.
- 必须包含：Visible pain point or danger.；External pressure that forces action.
- 避免：Do not open with static lore exposition.
- 结尾钩子：Stop on escalation, arrival, or a reversal about to land.
- 验收：The first third must include a concrete pressure source.

### Unit 2 core play
- 目标：Show the story's repeatable pleasure or central mechanism.
- 必须包含：A choice, clue, ability, relationship move, or tactical test.
- 避免：Do not solve the core problem with a free all-purpose answer.
- 结尾钩子：End with a first validation chance or higher pressure.
- 验收：The viewer should understand why this story can keep expanding.

### Unit 3 first payoff
- 目标：Deliver a small but real payoff while opening the next stage.
- 必须包含：A visible result from prior setup.；A new cost, enemy, field, or goal.
- 避免：Do not keep only setup with no return.
- 结尾钩子：Point toward a bigger situation or sharper cost.
- 验收：There should be a phase result without clearing all future tension.
## 全局必须
- Give a concrete viewing promise before expanding the world.
- Pay off at least one expectation by the third unit.
## 全局避免
- Do not override established character motivation for a cheap twist.
- Do not reveal the endgame mystery too early.
## 验收规则
- Every opening unit should contain visible conflict, pressure, or mystery.
- The third unit should provide a real emotional or plot return.
## 使用边界
- 这是创作方法参考，不覆盖项目事实、目标集数、目标时长和输出格式。
- 根据当前作品形态和具体任务灵活取用，不要生搬硬套。
